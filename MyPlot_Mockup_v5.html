My Plot

Your piece of ground and your plan, in two words. FarmVille verbs on an organizer's rhythm: seeds are first contact, watering is the follow-up on a 48 hour clock, blooms are permanent, and every bloomed flower makes fresh Praise daily. The chain runs one way: outreach earns ground, ground grows flowers, flowers make the Praise that climbs from Good Neighbor to Governor.

Sign Up: four fields, one promise
First name, last name, garden name, and date of birth. That is the whole account. The full name puts a real person behind every seed: friends see exactly who invited them ("Alex Johns planted a seed in your name"), which is what makes the invitation feel human instead of like spam. Every other gardener sees only the garden name. The date of birth is an age check.

Plant a Seed: a name is the only input, and no message on this screen
The player types a name to find the person they know. That is the entire form. Once a name is in, the Plant the Seed button lights up; tapping it kicks out to their own Messages app with the note pre-filled, and that is the first time they see the message. They edit it there and hit send themselves. Demographics come quietly from the voter file behind the curtain, the species is a surprise revealed by growing, and nothing about anyone ever shows on screen.

The Bloom: the cinematic, and the flower owns the screen
Triggered the moment Sarah taps the will-vote button. The bean cracks, a sprout breaks the surface, and the flower opens BIG, filling the frame. The species card lands below with its NEW ribbon (first-ever species only). Then every rolled trait pops up on the flower itself with a little gold burst: the butterfly flutters in, the bee lands, glitter glints across the petals. Dead last, a bonus banner: this bloom also dropped a new bed in your tray, shown only when a bed actually results. No praise numbers, no other plants, no updates. Refresh to replay.
