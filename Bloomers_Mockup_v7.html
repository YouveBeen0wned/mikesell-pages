Bloomers

Your piece of ground and your plan, in two words. FarmVille verbs on an organizer's rhythm: seeds are first contact, watering is the follow-up on a 48 hour clock, blooms are permanent, and every bloomed flower makes fresh Praise daily. The chain runs one way: outreach earns ground, ground grows flowers, flowers make the Praise that climbs from Good Neighbor to Governor.

Sign Up: four fields, one promise
First name, last name, garden name, and date of birth. That is the whole account. Friends who get a seed see the first name only ("Alex planted a seed in your name"), warm and familiar, the way a friend actually talks. The full name stays on the account and never shows to anyone. Every other gardener sees only the garden name. The date of birth is for Age Verification only.

My Garden, grown: no buttons, and shine has a hierarchy
The three rolled traits each mark a flower for good: Glitter Petals glints (Mom, Nina), the Sun Bloom gold rim (Sarah, Unc Ray), and Pollinators living on their petals (Unc Ray's bee). Only a gold-shelf flower earns the breathing glow (Ms. D). Nothing in this garden ever jumps or shakes: a water-ready seed sits still beneath a gently bobbing water drop with a soft ripple (JB, Gill), and a waiting seed wears a quiet countdown chip (Coach T). Below the garden sits one tool bar: the Shed holds earned beds (drag one out, drop it anywhere), the 👏 chip is the garden pool with a timer showing when the next daily Praise ripens, and Arrange (the trowel) grows in stages: moving single flowers between spots unlocks at Block Captain, then bed rearranging plus a bonus bed at Community Organizer. Underneath everything sits one clean 40px cell grid (the faint dots): every bed occupies whole cells, every plant sits on a cell center, and every drag snaps to it.

Plant a Seed: one question, three possible results
The page asks one thing: which of your friends would benefit from a vote reminder? The search then lands in one of three states. More than one match: a picker shows each person's side of town (a coarse area only, never an address) so the player taps the right one. Available: an animated celebration, then the one-action plant button with the editable name chip. No match: the same view in blue, a warm note that they may not be registered or in the district, a space for a cell number (used once to open the text, never saved), and the same one-action button carrying a registration-deadline reminder instead. Deadline seeds grow Wildflowers. Players who opt in to the contact compare (at sign up, re-offered every two rank-ups) skip the search box entirely: the page opens as a ready list of friends matched from their own phone, and picking one drops straight into the same celebration flow. Contacts are compared on the phone and never saved.
Phone one: a match found, with the picker and the celebration. Phone two: the contacts list for opted-in players. Phone three: no match, the deadline-reminder path.

The Bloom: the cinematic, and the flower owns the screen
Triggered the moment Sarah taps the will-vote button. The bean cracks, a sprout breaks the surface, and the flower opens BIG, filling the frame. The species card lands below with its NEW ribbon (first-ever species only). Then the three independently rolled variables pop up on the flower itself, each with a little gold burst: the ☀ Sun Bloom rim flashes on, the 🦋🐝 Pollinators fly in and land, and ✦ Glitter Petals spark across the petals. Any bloom can roll none, one, two, or all three; this one rolled the sweep. Then the bonus banner, shown only when a bed actually results. And dead last, the sky above the flower fills with cascading announcements, one line per rolled trait. No praise numbers, no other plants, no updates. Refresh to replay.
