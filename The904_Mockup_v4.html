The 904

A board, dice, and a sticker album made of Northeast Florida. Words are nearly gone: the game teaches by tapping. Texting a friend is the move that earns rolls, and a friend tapping back is the jackpot.

The Board: 24 tiles of home, one thumb, almost no words
County colors ring the board: emerald Duval, amber Clay, coral Nassau, blue St. Johns. Corners: Mike's Porch, the Spin, the Gift, and GO. Center fills with light as the district plays. Final art comes from Mike's own archive.

The other four variations, at a glance
Full write-ups in the Game Lab document. The 904 is the recommended chassis; each of these donates its best organ to it.
🎰🏗️
Spin City
Your slot machine as the whole game: spins on a timer, coins build your block, porch-drop gifting. Its spin became The 904's friend-dealer tile.
🌱🌸
The Garden
Your metaphor, playable: friends as seeds, taps as blooms, one shared district garden by November. Returns as an album chapter and a September season event.
🎱⚡
Tip the Table
"In the game of life, the table is tipped." A pinball toy where every friend tap fires the ball. Became the jackpot celebration.
🏮🌫️
Night Shift
Lamplighters, gone casual. Its finale survives: on election night the board's center district lights fully up.
